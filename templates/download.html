Column descriptions:

  • gene_id: unique identifier in the PG database.
  • article_title: title of the paper.
  • abstract: abstract of the paper.
  • unique_id: unique identifier in the database of the data source, such as entry in Uniprot.
  • sequence_source: which database does the data come from, such as Uniprot or NCBI.
  • gene_name: the name of the gene.
  • function_description: functional description of this gene.
  • species: the species from which the gene originates, such as Lactobacillus lactis.
  • exact_strain: the excat specie name from which the gene originates, such as Lactobacillus gasseri ATCC 33323.
  • doi: the doi number of the paper proving that the gene is a prebiotic gene.
  • amino_acid_sequence: the amino acid sequence of this gene.
  • nucleotide_sequence: the nucleotide_sequence of this gene.
  • Strict: have gene knockout experiments been conducted to prove whether the gene is a prebiotic gene.
