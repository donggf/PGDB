    Overview

    Perform standard BLAST searches against the PGDB reference sequences. Results are annotated with extra information from PGDB. Sequence data must be in FASTA format.

    qseqid: The sequence name uploaded by the user.
    sseqid: The name of the sequence in PGDB.
    length: The total length of the aligned parts of two sequences.
    pident: The percentage of precisely matched positions in the alignment section between two sequences.
    evalue: The lower the value, the higher the degree of matching.
